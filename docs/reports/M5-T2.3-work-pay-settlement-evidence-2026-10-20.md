# M5 T2.3 - Work pay settlement evidence (2026-10-20)

## Result

T2.3 turns completed regional work attendance into **cash wages** through the
same `FinanceManager` batch used by the Realm day pipeline. 16 physical work
ticks pay one work unit at 1 cash; a paid watermark per `(actor, region)`
prevents duplicate or transferred pay. The implementation and tests pass on a
combined dirty tree and were **accepted by Codex on 2026-10-20**. T2 and M5
remain open.

Plan: [M5-T2.3 work pay settlement](../design/M5-T2.3-work-pay-settlement-plan.md)
(approved by Codex 2026-10-20).

## Implemented behavior

- **Rate and floor**: `kAttendanceTicksPerWorkUnit = 16` and
  `kWorkWagePerUnit = 1` in `core/include/polyec/schedule.hpp`. Payment is
  `floor(regional_ticks / 16) - paid_work_units`; remainder ticks carry
  implicitly and never migrate between regions.
- **Regional state**: `ScheduleBook` stores `work_present_ticks_by_region` and
  `paid_work_units_by_region` as sparse `(actor, region)` maps. Only positive
  entries are stored.
- **Attendance credit**: cumulative unique physical Work presence is credited
  only while the actor is alive, not travelling (`destination == -1`), and the
  session region equals the actor's current region. Under the timeline
  contract the same credit increments the global and regional counters, so
  per-actor regional sums equal the global attendance counter.
- **Payroll timing**: work during day D pays at the next completed day's
  payroll; there is no intraday pay. `day_settlement` remains bookkeeping only
  and does not pay or advance wage watermarks.
- **Ordering**: staged batch groups are region ascending, private employer
  before public employer; claims within a group are actor ascending with
  `work_wage` (priority 0) before `role_topup` (priority 1) for the same actor.
  Role top-ups never advance a work-pay watermark.
- **Amount and source**: one cash per paid unit from the employer account chosen
  by job kind (`job_kind == 1` is public, otherwise private). Death freezes and
  forfeits a pending gap; later days do not pay it.
- **Shared account effects**: role top-ups remain the same 20-cash, 30-day,
  non-hired non-Civilian claim with unchanged routing/order, but their paid
  share can fall when work wages share an underfunded account. The separate
  `day_people` appointed-office salary keeps its logic, eligibility, amount and
  ordering, but work wages paid earlier on the same day can consume the public
  treasury it needs and end the office. Legacy `timeline_contract == false`
  payroll and appointed-office outcomes are unchanged.
- **Save schema**: `POLYEC_REALM_10` / `SCHEDULE_4` serializes both regional
  maps after the global counters. Records are ordered by `(actor, region)` for
  stable bytes/fingerprints. `v9`, `v8` and `v7` reads preserve the legacy
  global attendance and settlement exactly, start both regional maps empty,
  and therefore produce no retro-pay; later ticks restore exact increment
  parity and pay normally.
- **Load validation**: actor/region bounds, record count bound
  `people * (regions + 1)`, duplicate `(actor, region)` rejection, positive
  ticks, units not exceeding regional attendance, per-actor regional sums not
  exceeding global attendance, paid-without-tick rejection, and non-empty maps
  under `timeline_contract == false` are rejected atomically. Actor IDs may
  repeat in different regions.
- **Python binding**: read-only `work_wage_per_unit`,
  `work_present_ticks_by_region(actor, region)` and
  `paid_work_units_by_region(actor, region)`; no writer is exposed.
- **Unchanged**: action/`Intent`/mask/legality, observation, `realm_obs` /
  `realm_state`, `model_contract`, bundles/training and day-production rules.

## Test coverage added

- **Thresholds and regions**: 15 -> 0, 16 -> 1, 31 -> 1, 32 -> 2; split
  8+8 and 15+15 -> 0; 16 in A + 16 in B -> two independent claims.
- **Timing**: no intraday wage; the next day pays a completed block; no
  retro-pay when the timeline is enabled after legacy attendance.
- **Replacement**: an active Work assignment with no typed Work session pays
  no wage and credits no regional attendance.
- **Shortage and retry**: the employer firm starts payroll with exactly 1 cash against 2 earned units; the first payroll pays 1 and records watermark 1. The fixture restores the source to exactly 1 cash with a value-conserving save patch; the next payroll pays the remaining unit and records watermark 2. A later payroll does not duplicate pay; actor cash, total money and invariants are checked throughout.
- **Death with an unpaid gap**: after the same 1-of-2 partial payment, the actor is marked dead while 1 earned unit remains unpaid. The firm is restored to 1 cash, then two later payroll days verify attendance stays 32 ticks, the paid watermark stays 1, and the dead actor receives no deferred wage; total money and invariants are checked.
- **Role top-up**: same 20-cash claim, 30-day cadence, routing and order does
  not advance the work watermark; a fully funded office salary still pays.
- **Shared office salary**: at the day-30 boundary a one-unit work wage can
  leave the public treasury below the 20-cash salary, ending the office; the
  control world with the same top-up/tax effect pays the salary and keeps the
  office.
- **Batch payroll**: deterministic group/claim accessors, staged underfunding
  equals sequential groups, running-overlay claims (paid 20 then 10; cash
  effects 18 then 9 after tax), stale read-set atomic rejection, and prepare
  failure leaves inputs unchanged.
- **Save/load**: v10 round-trip preserves regional ticks, global attendance
  and watermarks; duplicate actor IDs in different regions round-trip;
  duplicate `(actor, region)` pairs reject. v9/v8/v7 migrations preserve the
  legacy global attendance/settlement exactly, start regional maps empty, and
  post-migration ticks restore parity and pay normally. v8 pays only the new
  32 ticks (2 units).
- **Corruption**: count over bound, invalid actor/region, duplicate pair,
  zero ticks, paid units over attendance, paid without ticks, per-actor
  regional sum over global, non-empty under `timeline_contract == false`, and
  zero-attendance normalization including the regional record.
- **Python**: read-only regional accessors match C++ at checkpoints, including
  paid/foreign values; no writer is exposed.

- `core/tests/test_schedule.cpp` v8 migration now expects the 32 post-migration
  ticks to pay 2 units instead of an empty regional map; the legacy global
  attendance/settlement remain preserved. This follows the approved plan's
  post-migration parity rule.

  explicitly (paid shares and post-tax cash), replacing assumptions that every
  group is independently funded.

  outcome under the timeline contract instead of requiring the salary to remain
  unchanged. Legacy `timeline_contract == false` outcomes are unchanged.
- No scenario fingerprint or tracked save fixture changed.

## Limitations

- Windows only; no Linux, app, Godot, native-runtime or training verification.
- Wages are a fixed policy rate (1 cash per 16 ticks), not a labor-market
  equilibrium or output-based price.
- Role top-up and appointed-office outcomes can change only because they share
  an underfunded account with work wages; their own eligibility and rules were
  not changed.
- T2 and M5 remain open. This evidence is not acceptance and does not update
  `docs/CURRENT_TASK.md` or `docs/DEVELOPMENT_HISTORY.md`.
- No commit, push, publish or training was performed.

# M5.2 — GeneralLuxury: sản xuất giỏ hàng xa xỉ và sự kiện riêng

Status: selected as the next missing GoodId after FinePorcelain. The trial rules below are fixed before implementation and are not a claim that the economy is balanced.

## Baseline and purpose

Current contract is `realm-v40`, save73, household/config and timeline/config v51, 382 state values,400 candidate actions,5,182 observation values and reward v14. GeneralLuxury26 has no specific producer or consumer. Tea, Incense, Silk, Gold, DecorativeGlass and FinePorcelain already have their own uses, so a general luxury bundle must consume its component goods instead of leaving them in stock for their previous uses.

Implement a `GeneralLuxuryAtelier` industry ID25 in Atlas region12. It converts a small, explicit basket of existing goods into a separate packaged event set. The three inputs—Tea, Incense and Silk—are all consumed in the same production transaction. The output is three GeneralLuxury items; the consumer represents a larger private event than single-item Tea, Incense or FinePorcelain use.
